<objective>
Finish the phase: the `people` product in the same list — accounts rendered as one-row directory entries — the proof
that the list machinery is entity-agnostic, and the stored golden frames that pin both new surfaces.

Purpose: DISC-02, and the phase's own premise. The account list adds no machinery: it is 05-01's reducer and the walk
Phase 4 extracted, instantiated over accounts instead of posts, which is why the tests here drive the same
selection, pagination, dedupe, stall, single-flight and back-stack assertions over both kinds and why a second
pagination path, dedupe set or stall rule would fail them.

Output: `SearchProduct.People`, `UserPage`, the port's `SearchPeople`, the account-item reader over the shared
instruction walk, the captured people fixture, the fake's people pages and adversarial set, the account row and the
`u` row, the row-kind dispatch that opens a profile from an account row, the two agnostic proof tests, and the
phase's golden corpus with its audit.

Not in this plan: any second list implementation (a defect, not an alternative), the `Media` product (deferred), any
account detail beyond the row (the profile is 05-02's frame, reached from here), and any change to the search posts
path 05-01 landed.

Requests (bounded, and each one named): `u` spends 0 before `enter`; a submitted people query costs exactly 1
`SearchTimeline` request under the `People` product; `enter` or `a` on an account row costs exactly 2
(`UserByScreenName` then `UserTweets` — 05-02's path, unchanged); reaching the last loaded account costs exactly 1
`SearchTimeline` request at the stored cursor (single-flight); `s` on a people frame costs 0 and says
`nothing to switch`.

</objective>
